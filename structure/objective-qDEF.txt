GOAL
====
Three parts in dependency order: the threshold needs the bank's size, and the volume needs
both. Each names the claim in ~/gw-minimal-match/structure/claims.yaml it serves.

D. Template banks and covering   (covering-at-mm097)
   1. Placement, pre-registered. Place banks at minimal match MM in (tau_0, tau_3) with the
      method of Cokelaer 2007: each cell uses the metric at its own position, and the lattice
      grows cell by cell to the boundary of the region. Do this for a hexagonal lattice, and
      for a square lattice aligned with the local eigenvectors with Owen's spacing (Owen
      1996, Eq. 3.16: the worst point of a square cell, its centre, then has mismatch
      exactly 1 - MM). Both lattices are spaced for the same covering radius, i.e. the same
      MM.
   2. Boundaries, pre-registered. Cells that fall on the non-physical side of eta = 1/4 are
      projected onto the eta = 1/4 line along the local eigenvector direction. Templates that
      lie outside the mass box but are physical are kept when they cover its borders.
   3. Sensitivities, reported apart from the main result:
      (s1) a global metric anchored at a predefined point -- m1 = 12.5, m2 = 6.25 Msun for
           the main region, m1 = 40, m2 = 20 Msun for the extended region -- instead of the
           local metric;
      (s2) non-physical cells discarded instead of projected.
   4. Give N_templates(MM) for both lattices and both regions, and the ratio
      hexagonal/square against the constant-metric expectation 0.770.
   5. Covering, by Monte Carlo over injected TaylorF2 signals: the fraction whose best match
      in the bank falls below MM, per lattice, with its Monte Carlo uncertainty. Report
      separately interior injections and border injections -- border meaning within one
      covering radius, sqrt(1 - MM) in local metric distance, of any boundary of the region.
      At equal nominal MM, compare each fraction with the ideal geometric expectation, and
      determine whether any excess comes from metric variation, the quadratic approximation
      (use q1bC's validity map), the boundaries or the implementation.
   MM = 0.97 is the headline point; report curves over a range of MM.

E. Detection threshold   (detection-threshold)
   Pre-registered operating point: false-alarm rate FAR = 1 / (100 yr) over an observation
   time T_obs = 1 yr. The false-alarm probability is FAP = 1 - exp(-FAR * T_obs) exactly;
   0.01 is only its approximation. Sensitivities, reported apart:
     - FAR = 1 / yr, as a permissive operating point. It is not a detection significance
       and must not be described as one.
     - 5 sigma, defined by its global probability: FAP = P(N(0,1) > 5) = 2.8665e-7
       (one-sided). Do not import a years-equivalent from any published event.
   1. Take the single-sample tail from q1bC (P(|z| > r) = exp(-r^2/2), verified there).
   2. The statistic is maximised over time and over the bank, so trials are correlated.
      Estimate an effective rate of independent trials nu_eff on manageable simulated
      segments of Gaussian noise filtered through the bank -- do not simulate a year -- and
      extrapolate N_eff = nu_eff * T_obs. Show the estimate is stable in segment length and
      compare it with the naive bound N_templates x (samples per second) x T_obs.
   3. Solve rho* from FAP = 1 - exp(-N_eff * p(rho*)) with p the single-sample tail.
   4. Close the loop MM -> N_templates (from D) -> nu_eff -> rho*. Report rho* at fixed
      nu_eff and closed-loop, over the MM range, for the operating point and both
      sensitivities.

F. Volume   (volume-loss-rule)
   Keep these separate; never merge them into one number:
     - 1 - MM^3: the exact worst-case fractional volume loss at fixed threshold, for
       sources uniform in Euclidean volume;
     - 3(1 - MM): its linear expansion;
     - 1 - <M_bank^3>: the population-average discretisation loss, from D's injections.
   Present separately: TaylorF2 injections (pure discretisation); IMRPhenomD injections, for
   which M_bank is the direct best match against the whole TaylorF2 bank (template-family
   error plus discretisation); fixed threshold; closed-loop threshold from E; interior and
   border injections.
   Then the optimum. Define the effective volume, up to a constant, as
       V_eff(MM) = <M_bank^3> / rho*(MM)^3 ,
   with rho* closed-loop from E. Find the MM that maximises V_eff, with its uncertainty,
   for TaylorF2 and for IMRPhenomD injections, in each region. A rough estimate
   (rho*^2 ~ 2 ln N_eff, N_templates ~ (1 - MM)^-1 in two dimensions) suggests that between
   MM = 0.97 and 0.99 the threshold increase costs about as much volume as the finer bank
   recovers. That is a hypothesis to test, not a result. V_eff ignores noise fluctuations of
   |z| near threshold; say how much that could matter, using q1bC's Rice distribution.

Every part reports the main region and the extended region separately (see SETTING). The
main region carries the headline numbers.


SETTING -- these choices are fixed, do not revisit them
======================================================
Source of truth: ~/gw-minimal-match/wiki/conventions.md. In brief:

- Detector and noise: one detector, stationary Gaussian noise, one-sided PSD
  `pycbc.psd.aLIGOZeroDetHighPower`, analytic.
- Band: f_low = 20 Hz, f_high = 1024 Hz. Segment: next power of two above the longest
  TaylorF2 duration in range; df = 1 / T_seg.
- Masses: m1, m2 in [5, 50] Msun, m1 >= m2, no spin. Two regions, always reported apart:
    main      M = m1 + m2 <= 35 Msun -- the inspiral low-mass search range in FINDCHIRP
              (Allen et al. 2012, Sec. I);
    extended  35 < M <= 100 Msun -- where TaylorF2 cut at ISCO stops being usable.
- Coordinates: chirp times (tau_0, tau_3) at f_0 = f_low = 20 Hz. The metric is not
  near-constant in them, not even in the main region; that is why placement is local.
- Templates: TaylorF2 (3.5 PN phase), ending at f_ISCO = 1 / (6^{3/2} pi M). IMRPhenomD to
  f_high is the physical signal in part F.
- Inner product <a|b> = 4 Re int a b* / S_n df. Match: normalised inner product maximised
  over time and phase. mu = 1 - match; M is total mass; no calligraphic M for match or
  mismatch.
- Analytic metric: the 3.5PN projected-phase metric by Owen's method, as frozen by q1bC.
- Placement, boundaries, sensitivities and interior/border split: as in part D.
- Threshold operating point and sensitivities: as in part E.
- MM = 0.97 is the headline; curves are functions of MM.


FIGURE RULES
============
Apply to every figure you make.

F1. Measurement against prediction. A figure whose y-values are computed from its x-values
    by the formula under test is not evidence. Plot what was measured against what was
    predicted, or plot the residual.
F2. Show the claimed precision. If the claim is agreement to X %, plot the relative error on
    a scale where X % is visible, not two overlaid curves.
F3. Do not collapse dimensions silently. Quantities that depend on (m1, m2) get a 2-D map, or
    are marked by mass ratio. Axes carry units.
F4. Every figure states how it could fail: its caption says what it would look like if the
    result were wrong. A figure for which that sentence cannot be written does not go in
    the report.
F5. No figure from another job's out/ is reused as an image.
